CLCTN · TYPE SYSTEM · VIDEO MEDIA · OCTOBER 2026
PROPOSAL
BUILT ON SUBTYPES (48)
One film, eight shapes of plastic.
A VHS tape, a LaserDisc and a 4K disc of the same film are read the same way on a shelf: title, director, edition, cut. What differs is the object: how many sides, which region lock, whether the tape is going to grow mould. Video Media is one type with eight subtypes. The parent holds the film and the packaging. Each format adds only what its own collectors ask about.
00 · THE PARENT TYPE
Everything here applies to every format, or to enough of them that it failed test 2 and moved up. Signed and Bundle of come from the item level (48 · 00) and are not repeated.
