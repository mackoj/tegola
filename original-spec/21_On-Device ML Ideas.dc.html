CLCTN · IDEA BACKLOG · ON-DEVICE ML · NOT YET COMMITTED
Little brains we might borrow.
Desert Ant Labs ships small on-device models as drop-in SDKs for iOS, Android and web. Three of the seven are interesting to CLCTN, one of those is interesting enough to plan around, and the rest are noise for a collection app. Nothing on this page is a decision — it is a page of maybes kept in one place so the C2C conversation does not restart from zero.
The reason it is worth a page at all is the commercial shape: on-device means no marginal server cost, no data leaving the phone, and it works offline — which is the same near-zero-infra posture the Technical Blueprint already commits to. A cloud moderation or PII API would break that posture; this does not.

02 · REDACT — THE ONLY ONE WORTH PLANNING AROUND
The obvious framing is privacy hygiene. The framing that actually justifies the integration is revenue protection: the day CLCTN takes a cut of a sale, every buyer and seller has an incentive to move the deal off-platform, and the way they do it is by typing a phone number into a chat box. Redact catches that at the keyboard, on the device, in 24 languages.
This is the leak Vinted spends real money fighting. Doing it on-device is strictly better than doing it server-side: it is cheaper, it works before the message is sent rather than after, and it means the PII never reaches our infrastructure — so the GDPR answer stops being "we process it carefully" and becomes "we do not process it."
