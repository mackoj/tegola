RECOMMENDATION
Launch B. Add C's checking service for expensive items once disputes data exists. Keep A as the plan if volume grows past the members.
B gives subscribers a reason to subscribe, keeps buyers and sellers among people who already care about condition, and limits the support work while the anti-scam tools are new. On small sales, Stripe's fixed costs and the €2 seller fee use up most of the commission (A loses money at €20), so each option needs a minimum fee. B's €3 minimum keeps every sale above zero.
03 · PREVENTING SCAMS
