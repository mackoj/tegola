# Milestone 1 · Foundation: report

**Status:** done on 2026-10-31, on `claude/tegola-rearchitecture-jcq29f`; merges into `main` with this report.
Scope: `tasks/plan.md` M1 (PLAN §11).

## What works

- **The repo skeleton**, copied from telaio and renamed:
  - configs: `.swift-format`, `.periphery.yml`, `.gitignore`;
  - scripts: `test.sh`, `ci.sh`, `secret-scan.sh`, `periphery.sh`, `sync-strings.sh`, the xcstrings tools;
  - new scripts: `test-linux.sh` (the pure core anywhere), `lint-api.sh` (the lookup contract) and
    `generate-xcodeproj.py`;
  - CI: `linux-core` on every push and PR, and `macos` by hand.
- **`TegolaCatalogue`** reads and validates all 18 type files. The four v1 types are enabled (D-23). An unknown field
  kind is an error, not a silent fallback (D-30). Choice fields that get their options from subtypes are understood
  (D-29).
- **`TegolaModel`**:
  - money as integer minor units with a currency, totalled per currency and never mixed;
  - place paths ("Cellar › Rack 3 › Bin 12") with near-duplicate detection;
  - the five status swatches;
  - the tab bar's rules (Shelf and Scan fixed, the other three movable, none hidden).
- **`TegolaInterchange`**'s delimited-text reader:
  - reads the real `Jeux collection.csv` (UTF-8, CRLF, semicolons, 21 columns, 485 games);
  - handles quoted cells, cells spanning lines, byte-order marks, UTF-16, and Windows-1252.
- **`TegolaLookupAPI`**:
  - generates its client from `api/tegola-lookup.openapi.yaml` with swift-openapi-generator, with no warnings;
  - its contract tests send recorded tegola-server responses through the generated client, covering the barcode
    lookup, batch matching, and a 429 with `Retry-After`.
- **`TegolaDesignSystem`**:
  - `TegolaDesignTokens` holds the archive's palette, 41 theme roles, the Board theme, and the type, spacing, radius,
    border and shadow scales;
  - every one of the 26 promised text/background pairs reads at WCAG AA in the Board theme;
  - the SwiftUI side has the theme in the environment, colour and font resolution, and font registration, with the
    three OFL fonts and their licences.
- **Guard tests** over every source file:
  - no `Date()`, `UUID()`, `Locale.current`, `Calendar.current` or `URLSession.shared`;
  - no `print`;
  - no colour literal outside the tokens, and no system colour;
  - no `.public` log interpolation;
  - the pure packages and the tokens import no Apple UI framework;
  - each rule also proves it catches its own sample.
- **The app shell:**
  - `Tegola.xcodeproj` with the app, `TegolaTests` and `TegolaUITests`, bundle id `fr.macko.tegola`, iOS 26.0;
  - `AppFeature` with the five tabs, where Scan presents;
  - `LaunchArguments` for `--reset`, `--seed` and `--screen`;
  - a TestStore test, a unit test and a UI test.
- **Docs:** `DECISIONS.md` (D-1 to D-33), `GLOSSARY.md`, `testing/ui-tests.md`, `tasks/HANDOFF.md` and the two briefs.
