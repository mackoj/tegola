## What works

- **The lookup client** (T6.8, D-73). `LookupClient` wraps the generated client, and features see only model
  candidates, mapped by the type's own fields.
  - Every call is signed with App Attest. A key is registered on first use, and once more after an unknown-key
    `401`. The key id is in the keychain (`mackoj/swift-keychain`). Signing runs one call at a time.
  - Debug builds read `TEGOLA_LOOKUP_URL` and `TEGOLA_DEBUG_TOKEN` from the scheme, and send the token instead.
  - `429`/`503` hand the request back with its wait. Offline or cancelled costs nothing. Other failures spend one of
    five attempts.
  - The worker drains the queue whenever the network is there. Each answer is kept on its request for Review.
- **Capture** (T6.9, F6). Scan opens the camera, VisionKit's live scanner reading barcodes and text.
  - **Add to** picks the type, then the collection Add by hand would.
  - **Save this one** adds an object before any network, with what was read queued in the same write. The camera
    stays open, and the object goes to the tray.
  - **Identify?** sends this shot's photo (2048 px, at most 2 MiB, no metadata) with what was read, and is off again
    for the next. It's never queued (D-71).
- **Bring your list** (T6.10, F9, D-74). From Add's **Import a list** or the empty shelf.
  - The best importer reads the file; **Something's off** offers the others.
  - The one sentence ("485 games on 20 shelves, into a collection called Jeux") offers a rename.
  - **Build my shelf** is one import batch, every row unconfirmed. The payoff counts what the notes said and offers
    **Undo**.
  - Matching runs after, 50 rows a call, outliving the sheet. Its answers wait for Review.
- **Review** (T6.11, F7, F8, D-75). Every unconfirmed object, grouped strong, to look at, and as written, with the
  reasons and the source.
  - **Confirm** fills only what's empty, from the lookup, then confirms. **Confirm all** takes strong matches only.
  - A row with two answers asks "Which one?". With nothing picked, it's confirmed as written.
  - "You already have this one" is said unprompted, with **Open yours**; nothing is merged.
  - The Today strip's *N to tidy* opens it.

## Review

A separate review agent read the three commits against F6–F9, the contract and D-73 to D-75. Its findings, all
fixed in `M6 review`:
- a cancelled or offline lookup spent an attempt, since the generated client wraps every error;
- **Confirm** on a "Which one?" row with nothing picked filled from the top candidate;
- each capture into a new collection made another collection;
- closing the import sheet stopped matching;
- Identify's JPEG wasn't held to 2 MiB, and what was read went out twice when Identify was on;
- two signed calls at once could register two keys, or reach the server out of counter order;
- undo could run twice; the tray opened the object, not Review.

Left as they are, and noted:
- a `4xx` refusal spends attempts like any failure, so it ends in *needs details* after five;
- a sheet asked for during the 0.6 s before the next one opens could stack.
