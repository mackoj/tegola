## What works

- **People read models** (T7.1, D-77, `TegolaModel/People.swift`):
  - `Activity`: loans out (overdue first, then the soonest due, then no date), what came back, who holds what, and
    what's borrowed in. Its `badge` is the overdue count, nil at zero.
  - `PersonRecord`: what they hold, what they lent you, every loan both ways, *came back n of m*, the average days
    out. With no history it invents nothing.
  - `Shelf.recentPeople` for the pickers, most recently used first.
- **Returns** (T7.2, D-77): how it came back (*same shape*, *damaged*, *something missing*, or not said) and a
  trimmed note, on the loan and the `returned` event. Events written before M7 decode unchanged. The history reads
  *Back from Dara, closed by you · damaged · Sleeve torn*; a give-back reads *Given back to Anne*.
- **Reminders and the nudge's facts** (T7.3): `Shelf.loanReminders` (one per open loan out on its due day, today
  included; none for returned, undated or overdue loans; at most 14, so with the clocks' 50 they fit the system's 64)
  and `Nudge` (title, person, days out, days overdue: nowhere to put money).
- **Share cards** (T7.4, T7.9, D-79): *the record* shows the spine's subtitle and year and the type's `auto` fields
  (the catalogue's facts); *my copy* adds the collector's other fields, the ladder's grades in order, and the photo;
  an asking price only with money on. No money value of any kind reaches a card, by construction and by a property
  test over every v1 type. `ShareCardView` draws it; the object's **Share** sheet sends it as an image, and Nudge
  sends the record card with its line. `WishlistCard` is ready for M8's wishlist screen.
- **Lend and Return** (T7.5, boards 18 and 20): to whom (recent first, or **+ New**), back by (2 weeks by default,
  1 month, a date, none), an optional note, **Hand it over**; how it came back, a note, *what gets written*, **It's
  home**. A refusal stays on the sheet. Someone else's object gets **Whose is it?** (the same sheet, borrow mode) and
  **Give back**. The object's sheets are one `Destination`.
- **The Person page** (T7.6, board 19, `Route.person`): the header, *Came back* and *Average out* tiles (`StatTile`,
  new in the design system), every loan with its chip, and **Lend them something** (a searchable list of what can
  go out, which becomes the lend sheet with them chosen).
- **Activity** (T7.7, boards 17 and 22): the tab's own stack; *Out now*, *Returned*, *By person*; loan cards with a
  footer that says how each stands (rust when overdue, yellow within a week) with **Nudge** and **Mark returned**;
  *In the house, not yours* with dashed rows. The tab's badge is the system's, the overdue count. The Today strip's
  *N overdue* line opens the tab.
- **Reminders and Nudge** (T7.8, D-80): loan reminders are scheduled with the clocks' (on accepting the offer and each
  time the Shelf appears) under their own `loan.` prefix; the offer also appears for a loan with a date. Nudge opens
  the share sheet with a friendly line: *Hi Dara! Just checking in on Homogenic: it was due back 7 days ago. No rush,
  tell me when.*
- **The system tab bar and shared actions** (D-78): SwiftUI's `TabView` with the five tabs in the collector's order;
  Scan still presents. `TegolaAction` in the design system holds every tab and every verb more than one screen says
  (words, SF Symbol, usual look); `ActionButton` and `ModalBar` read it, so each such word is one key in one catalog.
- French for every new string, with plural forms for day and loan counts.

## Review

A separate review agent read the branch against the M7 plan, F13, F14 and D-77 to D-80, without running anything.
It confirmed the main promises: no money reaches a card, a nudge or a reminder; loans of let-go, deleted or undone
objects never show; quantity objects never lend; every write is a command; every string has French. Its findings,
all fixed before the PR:

1. **A due date was a time of day**, so a loan lent at 09:00 went overdue at 09:01 on its day, and the card read
   *Overdue · 1 days* after 0. A loan is now due back on a **day**: the lend sheet stores the last second of it, the
   date picker starts today, and the overdue count is whole days.
2. **The due-day reminder repeated** each time the Shelf appeared after 10:00, because a passed reminder was
   rescheduled a minute later. A loan reminder whose morning has passed is now dropped, never sent late (the clocks
   keep their minute-later rule).
3. **My copy left out the condition** of every type without a ladder (records, books, watches…). It now carries the
   single grade.
4. **A returned loan could still send its reminder** until the Shelf next appeared. **It's home** now removes it.
5. **The nudge said "1 days ago".** The span is its own plural key now, in English and French.
6. **A card's data could hold an appraisal or a cost** inside a log's entries (drawn only as *n entries*). No list of
   entries goes on a card now; the property test checks it.
7. **Activity drew every Nudge card image on each redraw.** The card is now rendered only when the share sheet asks
   for it.

Kept as they are, with the reason: the Return sheet starts on *same shape* (board 20 does, and the collector sees it
before tapping **It's home**); after **Give back** the object stays on the shelf as someone else's and offers *Whose
is it?* again, which is right when it's borrowed a second time.
