# Milestone 5 · Launch types and the first piece: report

**Status:** done on 2026-11-01, on `m5-first-piece`; merges into `main` with this report. The model half (T5.1–T5.4)
merged with PR #14.
Scope: `tasks/plan.md` M5 (page 52, P3; PLAN F2; page 46).

**Exit test (page 52):** 5 of 6 testers finish onboarding with one complete object, and can say what the app will tell
them. That's a test with people, so it's the owner's; `docs/UX-FINDINGS.md` has the sheet for it. The automated
stand-ins:
- `FirstPieceFeatureTests` walk every path through the reducer, on a real in-memory database;
- journey 1 (`testTheFirstEvening`) runs from a cold launch: the app opens on the first piece, the piece is typed and
  given its story, and **One at a time** opens Scan for the second.

## What works

- **Describing a piece** (T5.5, `ExtractionClient`, D-72). A photo or a typed line becomes a name, a few words for
  what kind of thing it is, and a year. `Catalogue.guess(from:)` turns the words into a type (D-62).
  - On iOS 27, the on-device model reads the photo itself, with the text Vision reads from it.
  - On iOS 26, it reads Vision's text and the classifier's confident labels.
  - Without Apple Intelligence, a photo still gets Vision's labels as words, so a watch can be recognised as a watch.
  - Nothing leaves the phone. Photos, from the camera or the library, are re-encoded at 2048 px with no metadata.
- **The first piece** (T5.6, `FirstPieceFeature`), PLAN F2's four screens:
  1. **One question:** the live camera, **Type it**, **Library** and **Just look around first**.
  2. **Recognise, then ask why:** *Is this it?* with the name, type and year, and **That's it** / **Not quite**.
     *What is it?* has a dashed name field, a year, and type chips. **Next** says why it's disabled. *How did it come
     to you?* has five chips and a line, kept as the first history event and never as `acquired`.
  3. **2B:** a type that hasn't shipped is saved as a Thing that waits for it. **Count me in** records a tester.
  4. **The payoff:** the piece's signal under **ESTIMATE** ("Battery from 1998, usually lasts 25–35 years"), or "Not
     yet. Soon." with the month it changes; then the story, **One at a time** and **That's enough for today**.
  - The app opens on it while the shelf is empty and it hasn't been skipped. **Close** leaves it after the first
    screen.
  - If the shelf filled up meanwhile (sync), the piece is saved as an ordinary one rather than refused.
- **The empty shelf** (T5.7): **Scan your first object** opens the first piece again; **Type it** stays. **Bring your
  list** waits for M6's import.
- **Strings:** every new key has French, in the app's *vous*.

## Review

A separate review agent read the milestone against F2, D-63 and D-72. Its findings, all fixed before this report:
- a library photo was stored as it came, metadata included: it's now re-encoded like a shot;
- the model's year was written without the collector seeing it: it's now shown, and editable, before it's saved;
- without Apple Intelligence, typing a name lost the type the photo's labels gave: the guess now reads both;
- a refused save was a dead end, and a double tap could save twice: there's a fallback, **Close**, and a guard;
- the camera could keep running after leaving the screen early, and a double tap on the shutter lost a shot;
- Vision's labels weren't sorted by confidence before the top six were kept.

## Known issues and what's left

- **The camera beat isn't automated:** the simulator has no camera, so journey 1 types its piece. M6's scripted camera
  is where it joins.
- **The on-device model is untested on a device in this milestone.** The tests use recorded descriptions; the live
  client builds against the iOS 27 SDK, with its iOS 26 path behind an availability check.
- **Type names come from the type files, in English,** inside French sentences ("Les Watches…"). Localising type
  names is a catalogue change, for M9.
- **Owner:** the exit test with six people; D-72's readings (the story chips, *What is it?*'s type chips, and the
  sync fallback).
