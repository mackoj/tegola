# UX findings

What people did with Tegola in the exit tests with people (page 52). The owner runs them; each session gets a row,
and each finding a line under its milestone. Findings that change the product become D-entries or PD-log questions.

## M5 · the first piece

**Exit test (page 52):** 5 of 6 testers finish onboarding with one complete object, and can say what the app will
tell them. A fresh install, an empty shelf, no hint beyond "show it something you own".

**For each tester, note:**
- the device and whether Apple Intelligence was on (it changes what the camera recognises, D-72);
- what they showed it, and whether the guess was right (**That's it**) or they went to *What is it?*;
- whether they finished with one object on the shelf, with its story;
- what they said the app will tell them, in their words (the payoff);
- where they hesitated, and anything they said out loud.
